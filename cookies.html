Storage & Cookies Notice

DRAFT — Recheck the deployed application, hosting settings and all third-party scripts before publication. This notice must be updated if analytics, advertising, embedded media or additional tracking is introduced.

This notice describes browser-side storage currently apparent in the development application. It is not a guarantee that hosting providers or future integrations will not use additional technologies.

1. Essential authentication storage

The application uses the Supabase JavaScript client for sign-in and session management. The client stores authentication session information in browser storage so members can remain signed in. This is essential to account access and security. Members can clear this data through their browser settings, but doing so may sign them out.

2. Cookies and tracking

The current application source reviewed for this draft does not include Google Analytics or Meta Pixel scripts. No advertising or behavioural tracking cookies are intentionally configured by the application in this development version. Hosting, security, payment and infrastructure providers may use their own strictly necessary technologies when their services are used; their own notices also apply.

3. Future analytics and marketing

If analytics, advertising, social-media pixels, embedded video or non-essential tracking is introduced, this notice must be updated and an appropriate consent/preference mechanism must be implemented before those technologies are activated in jurisdictions that require consent.

4. Managing browser storage

Members can clear site data in their browser settings. Clearing authentication storage may end the current session. Browser controls differ by device and browser.
